## Modern work requires modern device management Intune replaces legacy tools with a cloud-first approach.

#### What Intune Delivers
- Device enrolment (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android)
- Security baselines
- Application deployment
- Conditional Access integration
- Remote wipe and compliance enforcement

#### Why Intune Matters
Devices are now:
- Remote
- Personal
- Cloud-connected

Intune ensures data protection without slowing users down.
My365Expert Tip: Intune works best when paired with Conditional Access and Zero Trust principles.
