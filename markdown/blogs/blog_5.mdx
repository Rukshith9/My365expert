## Zero Trust means never trust, always verify regardless of location.

#### Core Zero Trust Pillars
- Identity: MFA, Conditional Access
- Devices: Compliance-based access
- Data: Sensitivity labels and DLP
- Apps: Least privilege access

#### SMB Reality

You don’t need enterprise complexity you need clear policies and enforcement. My365Expert Tip: Zero Trust is a journey, not a single setting.
