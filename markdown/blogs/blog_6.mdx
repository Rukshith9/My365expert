## Most tenants grow organically and governance is added too late.

#### Common Issues
- Over-permissioned SharePoint sites
- No ownership model
- Uncontrolled external sharing
- No lifecycle management

#### The Fix
- Standardized Teams creation
- Clear data ownership
- Regular access reviews
- Retention policies

My365Expert Tip: Governance enables collaboration it doesn’t restrict it.
