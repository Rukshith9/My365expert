## Without governance, collaboration platforms become digital junk drawers.

#### Governance Essentials
- Naming conventions
- Team and site ownership rules
- External sharing controls
- Retention and archiving

#### Business Impact
Good governance:

- Improvement search
- Reduces risk
- Supports Copilot readiness
- Simplifies audits

My365Expert Tip: Governance should be invisible to users but powerful in the background.
